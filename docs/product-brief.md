PRODUCT
 
Name: Unwrap
 
Type: Web app
 
Purpose:
Unwrap allows groups of friends, families and coworkers
to organize gift exchanges and create wishlists.
 
Primary users:
18–40 year olds who regularly participate in birthdays,
Secret Santa and group gifting.
 
Core problem:
Organizing gift exchanges through WhatsApp becomes messy.
People forget deadlines, don't know what others want,
and struggle to coordinate anonymously.
 
Core features:
1. Create an exchange
2. Invite participants
3. Create wishlist
4. Add items to wishlist
5. Join an exchange
6. Draw recipient
7. View recipient's wishlist
8. Manage exchange deadline
9. Notifications
 
 
Platform:
Web (mobile-first).
 
Design objective:
The product should feel playful and social, but not childish.
It should feel trustworthy enough that users are comfortable creating accounts and sharing wishlists.
